# Faster Scale Recovery

Faster Scale Recovery is a specialized mobile application designed to empower individuals on their recovery journey using the FASTER scale. It combines daily self-assessment, behavioral tracking, and support system integration into a clean, modern interface.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📅 Dashboard & Interactive Calendar
*   **Consistency Tracker**: A visual 4-week window that highlights your check-in streaks and scheduled support calls at a glance.
*   **Interactive History**: Tap any past date on the calendar to instantly view your recovery stage and notes for that day in the summary card.
*   **Quick Check-in**: A prominent action button to launch your daily assessment immediately from the main screen.


### ✅ Daily Check-in System
*   **FASTER Scale Integration**: Full support for the FASTER scale (Restoration, Forgetting, Anxiety, Speeding, Ticked Off, Exhausted, Relapse) with built-in definitions for each stage.
*   **Automated Behavior Tracking**: Select common behaviors from dynamic chips that automatically append to your daily notes.
*   **Smart Notes**: 
    *   **Auto-Capitalization**: Sentences automatically start with capital letters for a polished writing experience.
    *   **Dynamic Sync**: De-selecting a behavior chip automatically cleans up the corresponding entry in your notes while preserving your manual thoughts.
*   **Call Verification**: Track whether you've made your scheduled support calls directly within the check-in dialog.

### 📞 Support Call Scheduler
*   **Weekly Recurring Alerts**: Configure your support call schedule once and receive high-priority notifications when it's time to connect.
*   **Contact Integration**: Seamlessly import support partners from your phone's address book.
*   **Actionable Alerts**: Notifications include "Call Now" (direct to dialer) and "View Schedule" buttons.
*   **Today & Upcoming**: Schedules are automatically sorted to show you who you need to call today first.

### 📜 History & Reporting
*   **Organized Archive**: Your journey is automatically grouped by "This Week" and then by Month for easy review.
*   **Expandable Review**: Keep your history list tidy with expandable entries that show full notes only when requested.
*   **Weekly Reports**: Generate clean, professionally formatted recovery reports for entire weeks to share with sponsors or groups via SMS or Email.

### 🎯 Commitment Tracking
*   **Weekly Goal Setting**: Set specific "Commitments to Change" with target completion counts per week.
*   **Progress Visualization**: An interactive checkbox system allows you to track your consistency throughout the week.
*   **Automatic Resets**: Start each recovery week fresh with a clean slate for all your active commitments.

### 📱 Home Screen Widgets
*   **Weekly Calendar Widget**: A 7-day snapshot of your recovery progress showing colors, check-in status, and call indicators.
*   **Quick Actions Widget**: Immediate access to Check-in, Call Schedule, and Commitments directly from your home screen.

*(Image: Widget Options)*

### ⚙️ Customization & Security
*   **Trusted Contacts**: Maintain a private list of support partners for quick communication.
*   **Flexible Calendar**: Choose between Sunday or Monday as your "Start Day of Week."
*   **Data Portability**: Full Backup and Restore functionality using secure JSON exports.
*   **Local Privacy**: Your sensitive recovery data stays on your device—never stored on external servers.
*   **Modern Theme**: Optimized for Material 3 with full Light and Dark mode support.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
*   **Language**: Kotlin
*   **UI**: Material 3, ViewBinding, ViewPager2, ConstraintLayout
*   **Database**: Room Persistence Library (Offline-first architecture)
*   **Concurrency**: Kotlin Coroutines & Flow
*   **Navigation**: Jetpack Navigation Component
